PART 1: THE ROOT CAUSES OF THE ROBOT FREEZING ("Stopping with fires left")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
During automated stress-testing across all presets and edge cases, we reproduced the
exact freeze reported by the user:

REPRODUCTION 1: Preset 3 (Warehouse 10x10) - Frozen at Step 31
- What happened: Fire spread to 12 active blocks in the warehouse.
- Priority Engine selected Cluster 4 as highest utility.
- However, all approach paths to Cluster 4 were blocked by walls and burning flames.
- find_safe_stand_pos() returned (None, None).
- In functions.py step():
  Because stand_pos is None, the agent executed NO action.
  It did not move, did not replan, and did not select an alternative reachable fire.
  On every subsequent tick, it picked Cluster 4 again and failed again.
  Result: Agent remained permanently frozen at position (2, 6) while 12 fires burned.

REPRODUCTION 2: Edge Case Blocked High Priority (testing/test_deadlock.py)
- Grid layout: Station at (0, 0), Fire A at (0, 3) [Priority 1], Fire B at (0, 5) [Priority 10].
- Fire A blocks the single corridor to Fire B.
- Priority Engine scores Fire B higher (Score = 25 vs Fire A Score = 4).
- A* to Fire B fails because Fire A is an active hazard.
- find_safe_stand_pos() returns None.
- Result: Robot freezes at (0, 0) on Tick 1 without moving.

REPRODUCTION 3: Delayed Spawning Cutoff Threshold
- In functions.py __init__, user-entered fires are randomly split: only 1 to 3 ignite at start.
- Remaining fires are queued in self.fire_sources with a random tick timer.
- If the agent clears the first fires quickly and returns to base, functions.py line 363 triggers:
  if (self.next_spawn_tick - self.ticks) > self.max_wait_limit:
      self.is_completed = True
- Result: Robot docks at base and announces "Mission Accomplished!" even though fires were left in queue!
